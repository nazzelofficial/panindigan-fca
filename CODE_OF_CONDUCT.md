Panindigan FCA Code of Conduct

Copyright (c) 2026 Panindigan

Our Commitment

The Panindigan FCA community is committed to providing a respectful, professional, and welcoming environment for everyone.

We value collaboration, knowledge sharing, and constructive communication. All contributors, users, maintainers, and community members are expected to interact with respect and professionalism.

We welcome participation regardless of:


Age
Background
Disability
Experience level
Gender identity and expression
Nationality
Personal identity
Race or ethnicity
Religion
Sexual orientation
Personal circumstances



Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:


Use respectful and inclusive language.
Treat others with professionalism and courtesy.
Respect different opinions, experiences, and approaches.
Provide constructive feedback.
Accept criticism gracefully.
Focus discussions on technical improvements and community goals.
Help create a positive and collaborative environment.
Respect the time and effort of contributors.



Technical Discussions

When participating in discussions, issues, pull requests, or reviews:


Focus on the problem, not the person.
Explain technical concerns clearly.
Provide helpful suggestions whenever possible.
Avoid unnecessary arguments or hostile communication.
Recognize that contributors may have different levels of experience.


Healthy disagreement and technical debate are encouraged when conducted respectfully.


Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:


Harassment or discrimination of any kind.
Personal attacks or insults.
Threats, intimidation, or aggressive behavior.
Trolling or intentionally disrupting discussions.
Publishing private or confidential information without permission.
Sexualized language, content, or unwanted advances.
Hate speech or targeted hostility.
Impersonating maintainers or representatives of Panindigan.
Any behavior that creates an unsafe or hostile environment.



Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:


Maintaining a respectful community environment.
Reviewing reports fairly and confidentially.
Taking appropriate action when violations occur.
Applying enforcement decisions consistently.
Protecting contributors from harassment or abuse.


Maintainers may modify discussions, remove content, restrict access, or take other actions necessary to maintain community standards.


Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all Panindigan FCA community spaces, including:


Source repositories
Issue trackers
Pull requests
Discussions
Documentation platforms
Community channels
Official communication platforms
Events or spaces where individuals represent Panindigan FCA


The Code also applies when someone represents the project publicly.


Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to: conduct@panindigan.com

When reporting, include:


A description of the incident.
Relevant links or evidence.
Date and location of the incident.
Any additional context that may help investigation.


Reports will be handled as confidentially as possible.


Enforcement Actions

Depending on the severity and circumstances, maintainers may take actions including:


Private warning
Request to stop certain behavior
Removal of inappropriate content
Temporary restriction from community spaces
Permanent removal from the project community
Revocation of contributor privileges
Other appropriate corrective actions


Serious violations may result in immediate action.


Security Reports

For security vulnerabilities or responsible disclosure related to Panindigan FCA, please contact security@panindigan.com.

Do not publicly disclose security issues before they have been reviewed and addressed.


Good Faith Participation

The Panindigan FCA community operates on trust, respect, and collaboration.

Contributors are encouraged to:


Ask questions.
Share knowledge.
Help others improve.
Report issues responsibly.
Contribute positively to the ecosystem.
